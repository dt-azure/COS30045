Traffic Safety and Law Enforcement

This report presents an in-depth analysis of road traffic law enforcement data. The primary goal is to uncover trends, identify violation patterns, and assess fairness in enforcement practices.

To clarify these complex aspects, the analysis is structured into three main narratives:

Story 1: The Landscape

The first section provides a broad overview of enforcement activities. It begins by examining enforcement trends over time, specifically monthly fine amounts - to determine whether enforcement is stable, seasonal, or influenced by specific events. Next, it analyzes the breakdown of offence types to answer the question: “What are the most common road safety violations?” Finally, it compares jurisdictions more fairly by normalizing fines per capita, rather than relying on absolute figures.

Story 2: Age and Bias

The second narrative focuses on human factors, particularly the relationship between age, violation type, and detection method. It analyzes violation patterns across age groups to see if different age groups tend to commit different types of offences. A key component is the analysis of detection methods (e.g., camera vs. direct police intervention), normalized by age, to examine whether certain age groups are more likely to be detected by specific methods. This analysis uses visualizations (heatmaps, box plots) to identify signs of fairness or bias in how age groups are treated.

Story 3: Fines, Arrests, and Charges

The final narrative delves into the severity of enforcement actions beyond fines. It examines all three indicators, arrests, and charges—over time to assess whether enforcement is becoming more punitive. Furthermore, it analyzes the proportion of each enforcement type and uses heatmaps to answer key questions such as: “Which age group is more likely to face punitive measures?” and “Are younger individuals charged more frequently than older ones?”

Through these three analytical stories, the report aims to provide a comprehensive and multidimensional view of the effectiveness and fairness of current traffic safety policies.
