This project was created to make Australia's road safety enforcement data more accessible, transparent, and meaningful. The Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE) recently expanded its datasets to include demographic and regional detail, opening the door to deeper insights into how fines, charges, and enforcement activities vary across age groups and jurisdictions.

While existing official dashboards focus mainly on totals, they often overlook questions of fairness, demographic patterns, and regional differences. Our visualization platform bridges this gap. It allows policy analysts, transport researchers, and planners to explore enforcement trends interactively—revealing which age groups receive the highest fines per capita, how detection methods differ across regions, and whether enforcement outcomes escalate from fines to arrests or charges.

By structuring the dashboard around real analytical needs—identifying disparities, comparing jurisdictions, and understanding enforcement behaviour. We aim to support clearer, data-driven conversations about road safety, equity, and policing strategies in Australia.
